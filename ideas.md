# AntPro visual brief

## Ground truth
The supplied screen recording is the reference. The recreation should feel like the same compact mobile portal: a 384px-wide handset composition with a thin olive header, tiny account and chip text, pale gray page surface, dense legacy web typography, and strong circular icon tiles.

## Design movement
Compact portal UI with deliberate information density. The visual movement comes from stacked screens and bold circular entry points rather than large imagery. Every screen should be immediately legible at phone width and remain usable at larger widths by centering the handset shell.

## Color philosophy
Use the recorded palette: #9d8b00 / muted olive gold for header and menu accents, #523016 / dark brown for actions, #ececec / pale gray page background, #ffffff surfaces, #111111 game surface, and bright green, red, cyan, blue, and orange tile accents.

## Layout paradigm
A centered mobile shell, full-width content bars, tight 6–10px gaps, two-column dashboard grid, narrow tables with tiny all-caps headings, and lower browser/navigation chrome to echo the recording. Desktop uses the same shell with more breathing room around it rather than changing the information architecture.

## Signature elements
The lowercase gold `antpro` wordmark, olive account strip, dark brown section buttons, circular portal icons with tiny labels, dense bookmaker/ledger tables, and the gold slide-out menu are the recurring signatures.

## Interaction philosophy
Taps should switch between recreated views, open overlays, toggle tabs, expand sections, and show inert demo feedback. Controls that could imply money remain explicitly simulated and never submit or process real transactions.

## Animation
Keep motion subtle and reference-like: quick slide-in menu, gentle view fade, and simple disclosure transitions. No flashy casino animation or attention-grabbing effects absent from the recording.

## Typography
Use a compact system sans stack (`Arial`, `Helvetica Neue`, sans-serif), bold uppercase labels, and small 10–12px body copy to match the source's dense mobile portal styling.

## Brand essence and voice
Functional, compact, operator-oriented, and unmistakably branded. Copy is terse and uppercase where the recording is terse and uppercase. Demo notices are neutral and clear.

## Wordmark and project icon
Use a CSS/text `antpro` wordmark in gold for the UI and a simple full-bleed olive square project icon with a gold lowercase mark for the platform favicon/metadata.
